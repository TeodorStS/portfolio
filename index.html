Featured · Full-stack · CS++ Society

jumpflip

A Flappy Bird-style recruitment game I designed, built and deployed for the CS++ Society. It was played by 90+ students across 1,500+ games at TU Dublin's society fair, and students signed up to the society along the way.

  • Owned it end to end in production: Docker containerisation, Traefik and Nginx/HTTPS routing, health checks and persistent storage.
  • Scores can't be faked: the browser sends only the player's inputs, and the server replays the run to work out the score.
  • Password-protected organiser dashboard with search and CSV export, so student numbers never appear on the public screen.
  • Node.js
  • Express
  • SQLite
  • Docker
  • Traefik
  • Nginx

04 — Album reviews

On the turntable

Short, honest write-ups of records worth your time.

MASTER OF PUPPETS

Metallica · 1986 · Thrash metal

Master of Puppets

5.0

The downpicking endurance test that built a genre.

Read the full review

Eight tracks, zero filler. The title track alone is a masterclass in arrangement — riff after riff stacked with intent, and the clean middle section is the best breather in metal history. "Orion" proves Cliff Burton was operating on another plane. If you only ever hear one thrash record, it's this one.

BLACKWATER PARK

Opeth · 2001 · Progressive death metal

Blackwater Park

4.5

Beauty and brutality merged into one 67-minute forest.

Read the full review

Nobody swings between crushing growls and fragile acoustic passages like Opeth, and this is where they perfected the formula. "The Drapery Falls" is the entry drug; the title track is the point of no return. Half a point off only because it ruined every lesser prog record for me.

THE JOY OF MOTION

Animals as Leaders · 2014 · Instrumental prog

The Joy of Motion

4.0

Eight strings, zero vocals, infinite humility for guitarists.

Read the full review

Tosin Abasi plays guitar the way the rest of us wish we thought about music. "Physical Education" grooves harder than most albums with drummers twice as loud. It loses a point only because listening to it while practicing is actively demoralizing.
